Hello Faces

The world is full of faces. A surprised outlet. A grumpy teapot. A closet that looks sad about something. Catch them, name them, and see which one makes everyone laugh.

Catch

Spot a face in an ordinary object, snap it, give it a name and a mood.

Judge

Two faces, one tap: pick the funnier one. Every vote moves the rankings.

Collect

Fill your album with favorites, complete mood sets, and climb from Bronze to Legendary.

Eight moods

Every face belongs to one mood: joy, rage, shock, sorrow, mischief, love, smug or worry.

😄😠😮😢😜🥰😏😟

Free, and fair

Hello Faces is free to play. Nobody can buy votes, rank or an advantage. Every catch is reviewed before other players see it, and anyone can report a face that shouldn't be there.
